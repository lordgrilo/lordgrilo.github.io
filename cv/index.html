Giovanni Petri

Professor of Network Science, Network Science Institute, Northeastern University London
Head of NPLab · Network Science Lead, Project CETI

giovanni.petri@nulondon.ac.uk ORCID 0000-0003-1847-5031 Google Scholar GitHub

I study how the structure of a system constrains what it can represent and do. My work connects networks, higher-order interactions and information theory with cognition, neural organisation and collective behaviour, and I work with experimental colleagues, from neuroscience to marine biology, to test which of these distinctions can be measured in living systems.
