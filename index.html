01 Structure

When does the whole
change the rules?

Some interactions only make sense in groups. I use topology and statistical physics to understand what becomes possible when we look beyond pairs.

02 Cognition

What is the cost
of sharing?

Shared representations let us generalise. They can also make tasks interfere. I study the limits this tension places on brains and artificial learning systems.

    About

    Physics, minds,
    and other lives.

    I trained in Pisa, did a PhD at Imperial College London, and spent years in Turin at the ISI Foundation and CENTAI. I now lead NPLab at Northeastern University London.

    I’m also a member of Project CETI, where I lead the network science. My work moves between brain activity, artificial learning systems and the social lives of sperm whales, following questions that rarely stay within one discipline.

    About this site

    Each section runs a small model in the background: coupled oscillators; simplicial contagion, where groups as well as pairs pass activity on; a recurrent neural network driven by five overlapping inputs, seen through its top two principal components; Couzin's three-zone model of collective motion, where groups gather, turn into rotating mills and stream; Brownian drift; and the wave equation, with two sources taking turns, each drawn on its own. Clicking the open space nudges whichever model is showing. They are illustrations, not research data. Positions in the work map are an editorial reading.
